# Hồn Việt

A searchable, browsable collection of Vietnamese folk sayings, digitised from a two-volume dictionary.

## Language

**Entry**:
One headword of the source dictionary (a single ca dao, tục ngữ, thành ngữ, …) together with its explanation. Its kind is recorded as its Category.
_Avoid_: Poem (most entries are not poems), saying, row
